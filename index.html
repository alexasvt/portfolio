Current Research Projects

Black Christians & technology

We seek to understand how Black Christian young adults between 18 to 25 years old use technology in their religious and spiritual practices. Black Americans are among the most religious racial and ethnic groups in the United States. However, Black young adults are increasingly identifying as less religious compared to older generations.

Ai Tools for Hospital Educators

Hospital teachers are certified specialized teachers in the hospital that provide instruction to children in grades K-12. Before the popularity of generative AI, these teachers would develop custom accessible lesson plans for the diversity of students admitted into the hospital. We seek to understand how generative AI tools can be reimagined to support teachers.
